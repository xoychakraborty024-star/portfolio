About

Crew — 1

I run PeakSanima, a Delhi-based studio directing and shooting campaign films and brand stills — the kind of work that sits somewhere between advertising and documentary, built on real light and real locations.

Three years in, I've moved between sets as a Director, an Assistant Director, and a DOP — a one-person crew with a camera bag and a hard drive when the job calls for it. Every job at PeakSanima gets treated like the film it could be, not just the deliverable it needs to be.

I've shot brand and campaign work for Hyundai, Stay Perfumes, The Kebab Factory, Cafe Tesu, Yes Minister, and Evens, alongside content shoots for creators like @wokenupsid and @eshaanduggal.

Available for commissions worldwide. Studio based in Delhi.
